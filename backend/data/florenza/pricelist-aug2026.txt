HOW TO CARE FOR PORCELAIN TILE
Porcelain tiles are manufactured using only the best raw materials to achieve its superior quality. They are produced through state-of-
the-art production facilities with a very high level of precision.
Superior quality combined with a wide range of products provides total beauty and freedom in designing any style of flooring, from
classical to contemporary, residential to commercial applications. Tiles will maintain a long lasting beauty through minimal
maintenance.

BEFORE INSTALLATION
Before installation begins, the installer is responsible for checking the tiles for any visual defects. Always verify the shades, caliber
and texture of tiles. Cartons of tiles must be blended to achieve aesthetic blending. If there is any visible stain on the tile surface,
clean it first to make sure that stain can be removed.

DURING LAYING
Porcelain tiles come in mono-caliber; therefore it is only necessary to leave a very small joint for trapped air to go through. We highly
recommend 1/8” joint and no less than 1/16” grout joint. Please refer to the correct installation materials and specifications as
recommended by Tile Council of America and major manufacturers of installation materials.

UNPOLISHED FINISH TILE
To protect unglazed porcelain tiles from grout staining and for easier grout clean up we recommend the use of a grout release

POLISHED & HONED FINISH TILE
To protect Polished and honed porcelain tiles from grout staining and for easier grout clean-up we recommend the use of a grout

DAILY MAINTENANCE
Remove all debris using an untreated dust mop, soft bristle broom or vacuum. Followed by damp mopping with clean hot water. Dust
mop floors frequently, as grit can act as an abrasive on a polished surface. Ongoing preventive maintenance will usually reduce the
need for any problem-solving maintenance. For moderately soiled floors, agitate the surface with a neutral cleaner. Avoid cleaning
solutions that contain oil, fats or acids. Follow manufacturer’s instructions for proper application and dilution of cleaner. Allowing the
cleaner to remain on the floor to properly suspend dirt, heavily soiled floors, may require a natural-bristle brush, nylon scrubbing pad
or flooring machine. Use a degreaser in food preparation areas. Remove the cleaning solution and thoroughly rinse with hot water
using a mop or wet-vac. Some cleaning solutions may require a second rinse to remove all residues. Towel dry or buff tiles to
prevent spots and streaks
